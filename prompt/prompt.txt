# Build "Personal Best": a 29-second animated PSA in Remotion

You are building a frame-accurate 2D animated video with Remotion (React). It is a 29.0-second public service announcement for the HOSA PSA event (topic: "Maintain My Brain: Practicing the Pillars of Brain Health"). Everything you need is in this spec: timing to the frame, every color as a hex value, every size in pixels. Where this spec gives a number, use that number. Where it describes something without a number, choose values that fit the style rules in section 4 and tell me what you chose.

**The story in one paragraph:** A teen "speedruns" Hilarity for Charity's five brain-health habits against a game-style timer in the top-right corner: phone into a drawer and lights out (Sleep), a no-look catch of an orange over oatmeal (Nutrition), sprinting up stadium steps (Physical Exercise), finally landing a hard guitar chord (Cognitive Fitness). The last split is calling his grandmother (Emotional Well-Being). He starts rushing the call, looks up at the timer, swipes the timer off the screen with his finger, and sits down on the kitchen floor to keep talking. The music cuts to silence. Then the game flashes PERSONAL BEST anyway.

## 0. Ground rules (read before anything else)

1. **Do not write any words that appear in the video** beyond the exact strings in this spec. Every team-written line comes from `src/teamText.ts` placeholders (section 8). Never fill those placeholders yourself, even with "example" text.
2. **Do not generate voices, dialogue or music.** The team supplies all real audio. You only generate clearly labeled `TEMP_` timing placeholders (section 9).
3. **No logos, brand marks, real app interfaces, real products, copyrighted characters, or imitations of any studio's art style.** Everything is drawn from scratch in SVG and CSS from this spec. No external images, stock assets or icon libraries.
4. **Deterministic rendering:** never use `Math.random()` or `Date.now()`. Use Remotion's `random(seed)` for anything that needs variation.
5. **Units:** all positions are in px in a 1920×1080 design space, origin at top-left, unless a section says otherwise. All times are in frames at 30 fps. "f195" means frame 195.
6. **Default easing:** `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)` unless a step says otherwise. All cuts are hard cuts except the whip-pan between shots 3 and 4.
7. **Work in milestones (section 12).** Start by showing me a short build plan. Pause after milestones 3, 6 and 9 to show me the review stills.
8. Ask before installing anything outside this project folder.

## 4. Global art direction

**Style:** flat vector illustration with soft gradient shading. No outlines. Realistic human proportions (7.5 heads tall), no exaggerated cartoon features, rounded geometry. Each shape gets one shadow tone (12–18% darker) on the side away from the scene's key light and one highlight tone (about 10% lighter) toward it. The goal is believable, real-life moments: it should read as illustrated realism, not a cartoon.

**Finishing layers on every shot, in this z-order:** scene art → vignette → grain → why-line / end card text → HUD / PERSONAL BEST.
- Vignette: `radial-gradient(ellipse at center, rgba(0,0,0,0) 55%, rgba(0,0,0,0.18) 100%)`.
- Grain: full-frame SVG `feTurbulence` (`baseFrequency 0.9`, `numOctaves 2`), new seed every 2 frames, opacity 0.04, `mix-blend-mode: overlay`. The HUD and text sit above the grain so they stay crisp.

**Handheld camera (shots 4, 6, 7):** deterministic shake with t in seconds:
`x = A·sin(2π·0.7·t + 1.3) + 0.5A·sin(2π·1.9·t)`, `y = A·sin(2π·0.9·t + 0.4) + 0.5A·sin(2π·2.3·t + 2.1)`, `rotation = 0.15·sin(2π·0.5·t)` degrees. A = 2 px in shot 4, 3 px in shots 6 and 7.

**Whip-pan blur:** apply an SVG `feGaussianBlur` with `stdDeviation="X 0"` (horizontal only) to the whole scene group, X ramping as specified in shots 3 and 4.

## 6. The HUD (`src/hud/`)

**Position (16:9):** absolutely positioned, `right: 96px; top: 64px; width: 440px`. Padding `22px 26px`, radius 16 px, background `C.hudBg`, border `1.5px solid C.hudBorder`, `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`. The panel spans about x 1384–1824, y 64–252.

**Rows, all right-aligned:**
1. Timer, 68 px tall line.
2. Margin-top 10 px: `[split time] [label] [8 HRS pill]`, items vertically centered, gap 12 px.
3. Margin-top 14 px: five pips, 16 px diameter, gap 10 px. Empty: transparent fill, 2 px stroke `C.pipEmptyStroke`. Filled: `C.gold` fill and stroke, `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(245,197,66,0.6)`.

**Timer text:** use integer math to avoid floating-point errors:

```ts
export const formatTime = (frame: number) => {
  const totalCs = Math.floor((frame * 100) / 30);
  const m = Math.floor(totalCs / 6000);
  const s = Math.floor(totalCs / 100) % 60;
  const cs = totalCs % 100;
  return `${m}:${String(s).padStart(2, '0')}.${String(cs).padStart(2, '0')}`;
};
```

The timer always shows the video's real elapsed time.

**Behavior by frame:**
- f0–3: fade in (opacity 0→1) and slide down (translateY −8→0).
- Labels: f0–149 SLEEP; f150–224 NUTRITION; f225–299 PHYSICAL EXERCISE; f300–389 COGNITIVE FITNESS; f390 onward EMOTIONAL WELL-BEING (never completes during the run).
- **Completion at frames 105, 195, 285 and 360:**
